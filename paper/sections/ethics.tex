\section{Ethics and responsible use}
\label{sec:ethics}

The study uses only information that players or their clubs have made public and that is published under open licences: Wikidata (CC0), Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and government statistics under the Open Government Licence. No individual is contacted or profiled, and the analysis reports counts of players per area. The released data contain each player's Wikidata identifier and birthplace as recorded on Wikidata, which is public, but no information beyond it. The results describe areas and should not be used to judge or stereotype places, communities or individual children; an area's low ratio says nothing about any boy born there. Scouting or investment decisions based on these maps should take into account that the coldspots may reflect fewer opportunities rather than less talent.
